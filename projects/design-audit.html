Inside IKC

An immersive organisational design audit at IKC Foods LLP

How ready is a 60-year-old family food brand for its next five years?

IKC Foods has grown on 60 years of trust, but its next chapter depends on how the organisation works every day. This audit follows the work across 11 divisions, from factory floor to store shelf, to see what people do versus say, and to shape it into opportunity areas for a future-ready IKC.
